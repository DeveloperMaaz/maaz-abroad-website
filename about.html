The backstory

From Islamabad to Hannover

I grew up in Pakistan dreaming about studying abroad. When I finally got the chance to move to Germany, I thought the hard part was over. Turns out it was just beginning.

Nobody told me that finding an apartment could take months. Nobody explained that Anmeldung has to happen within two weeks. Nobody warned me about the blocked account timeline or the right sequence for insurance. I made every mistake in the book.

Along the way, I started documenting what I learned. First for myself, then for friends back home, then for a growing audience of students who were about to make the same move. That's how Maaz Abroad started.

Today, beyond creating content, I sit down with people one-on-one and walk them through the exact same process. Not theory from a guidebook, but the real steps, in the right order, from someone who recently went through all of it.
