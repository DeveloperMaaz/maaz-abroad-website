Services

Everything you need to land and settle

One-on-one guidance for every stage of your move to Germany. No generic advice, no copy-pasted checklists.

🛂

Student Visa Guidance

I walk you through the entire student visa application step by step. From gathering documents to preparing for the embassy interview, you'll know exactly what to expect and what to avoid.

  • Complete document checklist for your country
  • Blocked account setup and timeline
  • University enrollment letter requirements
  • Embassy appointment preparation
  • Interview questions and how to answer them
💼

Job Search Visa Support

Planning to job hunt in Germany? I help you understand the eligibility criteria, prepare a strong application, and plan how to make the most of your 6-month window.

  • Eligibility check and qualification assessment
  • Application documents review
  • Proof of funds guidance
  • Job hunting strategy for the German market
  • Converting to a work permit after landing a job
🎯

Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)

Germany's new points-based immigration pathway. I help you calculate your score, understand the requirements, and put together an application that gets approved.

  • Points calculation and gap analysis
  • Qualification recognition process
  • Language requirements and proof options
  • Application filing and documentation
  • What to do after you receive the card
🏠

Accommodation Search

Finding a place in Germany as a foreigner is one of the hardest parts. I share the exact platforms, messaging strategies, and red flags I've learned from experience.

  • Platform-by-platform strategy (WG-Gesucht, ImmoScout, etc.)
  • How to write messages that get replies
  • Scam detection and red flags
  • Temporary housing options before finding permanent
  • Understanding rental contracts and Kaution
📋

Settling-In Support

Your first weeks in Germany involve a pile of registrations, sign-ups, and bureaucratic steps. I give you the right order and the exact process for each one.

  • Anmeldung (city registration) process
  • Health insurance selection and sign-up
  • Bank account opening
  • Phone contract and SIM card
  • GEZ, Rundfunkbeitrag, and other first-week tasks
